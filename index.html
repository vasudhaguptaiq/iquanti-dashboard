Consecutive months at this status counts how many months in a row, ending with the selected month, the project has had the same goal status. For example, a project that was Behind in Jul, Aug and Sep shows 3 for Sep. The count restarts when the status changes or when the project has no goal that month. Projects marked NA (no goal that month) are not listed or counted. Rows highlighted in red are projects that have been Behind for two or more months in a row.

ProjectPerformance observationInsight
How this table works. A project is listed if its margin fell by 10 or more percentage points versus the previous month (revenue of at least $5K in either month), is negative, or has stayed below 70% for two or more months. January has no earlier month, so projects below 70% or negative are listed instead. A percentage point is one point of margin: going from 78% to 68% is a fall of 10 percentage points.

How this table works. A project is listed if its Rev/FTE fell 25% or more versus the previous month, if hours grew 20% or more while revenue lagged well behind, or if it stayed below 70% of the portfolio Rev/FTE for two or more months (projects with at least 20 hours). January lists projects below 70% of the portfolio.
